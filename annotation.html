Interactive SVG Annotations

Click a Label to Jump to its Annotation

Rectangle: <rect>

Used for the house body, door, and car bodies. It's defined by its top-left corner (x, y), a width, and a height.

<rect x="50" y="50" width="200" height="100" fill="#a0a0a0" />

Circle: <circle>

Used for the moon, stars, and car wheels. It's defined by its center point (cx, cy) and a radius (r).

<circle cx="150" cy="100" r="50" fill="#f0e68c" />

Polygon: <polygon>

Used for the house roof. It's a closed shape defined by a list of x,y coordinate pairs in the points attribute.

<polygon points="50,150 250,150 150,50" fill="#4a3e3e" />

Line: <line>

Used for the road dashes. It's a straight line from a starting point (x1, y1) to an ending point (x2, y2).

<line x1="20" y1="150" x2="280" y2="50" stroke="white" stroke-width="4" />

Path: <path>

Used for the birds and pathway. A path is the most powerful shape, defined by commands in the d attribute. M means "Move To" a point. Q means "draw a Quadratic curve".

<path d="M 50 100 Q 100 50, 150 100" stroke="white" stroke-width="2" fill="none" />

Group: <g> with transform

Used for the cars, windows, and skyscrapers. The transform="translate(x, y)" attribute moves the entire group of shapes inside it without changing their individual coordinates.

<g id="car1" transform="translate(100, 80)"> ... </g>
